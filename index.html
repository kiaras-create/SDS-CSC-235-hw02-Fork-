Summary

The data used for these visualizations was collected by the Center for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) through the behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System. For this analysis, only data from Alabama was used, which limits the generalizability of the findings to other states or populations. This dataset is based on self-reported responses from individuals aged 18 and older and is designed to provide insight into physical and mental health and lifestyle factors.

However, there are multiple sources of bias. First, responses are self-reported which make answers very subjective so some individuals may overreport or underreport based on their individual perspective. Additionally, the survey only consists of answers from individuals aged 18+ which omits anybody younger than that. There is also potential nonresponse bias, since individuals can choose not to participate or skip certain questions. This may create a gap between actual responses versus those who did not respond. Finally, some responses with missing data were removed which may introduce selection bias, especially if the responses which were removed were not random.

The data was accessed from: 2024 BRFSS Data (SAS Transport Format) [ZIP – 64.3 MB]
